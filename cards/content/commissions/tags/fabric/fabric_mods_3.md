# Fabric Mods (3)

### Herobrines Like

Some weird things are happening in the world, you must convert the normal ores into a new variant, craft a new weapon and armor, explore for unusual variations in your world and try to fight against this dangerous creature called Herobrine.

Check more on: [[herobrines_like|Herobrines Like]]

### Hour Limit

Mod that limits the max playtime of all the players based on a specific value. Giving a lineal mechanic where each player enters after the last one, saving their progress and items. All this to make a video that is: 50 players have 1 hour of playtime each one, and they should finish the game.

Check more on: [[hour_limit|Hour Limit]]
