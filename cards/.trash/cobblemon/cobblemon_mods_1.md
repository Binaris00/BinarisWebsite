## Cobblemon Mods

List of mods that have compatibility in any way to Cobblemon, including new mechanics, replacing anything of the gameplay or working as compatibility bridges with other mods.

### Cobblemon Legendary Spawn

Add news config to modify how-when a custom list of Pokémons (Normally legendaries) will spawn, this handles things like flying/water spawn, special biome/weather conditions and probably things, all inside a simple and fast config.

Check more on: [[cobblemon_legendary_spawn|Cobblemon Legendary Spawn]]

### Cobblemon Raids

Invite and create a party of 3 players to start a raid!! Only 3 lives for team, each loss cost a live, and you need to fight through 3 Pokémon and then fight the boss (Normally a legendary/special Pokémon) with special abilities, after defeated you have the change to capture it. GUIs, config and everything was used here!!.

Check more on: [[cobblemon_raids|Cobblemon Raids]]
