# Fabric Mods (2)

### Cobblemon Raids

Invite and create a party of 3 players to start a raid!! Only 3 lives for team, each loss cost a live, and you need to fight through 3 Pokémon and then fight the boss (Normally a legendary/special Pokémon) with special abilities, after defeated you have the change to capture it. GUIs, config and everything was used here!!.

Check more on: [[cobblemon_raids|Cobblemon Raids]]

### Exploration Revamped

Adds several QoL features related to exploration and movement around your world. Trying to change a lot of mechanics and day to day features of the original game, specially focused on adding new items, crafting recipes and overriding default mechanics.

Check more on: [[exploration_revamped|Exploration Revamped]]
