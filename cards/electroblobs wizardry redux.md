# Electroblob's Wizardry Redux

Electroblob's Wizardry is a mod focused on finding and using spells freely and having artifacts and different things that let you customize your weapons and tools in ways involving the essence of magic itself. Some of the original features:

- 180+ spells, each with unique and special behaviors
- 8 different magic elements (fire, ice, earth, and more!!)
- Upgrade your wand to cast higher-tier spells.
- Discover 4 new elemental armor sets and over 70+ powerful artifacts.
- Interact with good wizards or challenge evil mages hidden throughout the world

After a lot of time in development, I have finally made an alpha version to show the mod to the public. This is coming with a big change of architecture to the original mod, now supporting multiple mods and remaking the internal system to be more accessible and easy to use in the long run.
