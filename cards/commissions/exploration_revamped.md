### Exploration Revamped

Adds several QoL features related to exploration and movement around your world. Trying to change a lot of mechanics and day to day features of the original game.

Some features:

Added 2 new types of rails: normal powered rail that let you go 17 blocks per second and super powered rail that let you go 25 blocks per second (this one needs 1 diamond for each rail).

Spyglass lets you zoom 4x + you can zoom in/out.

Villagers no longer reroll trades, now you need an item to do this (this could also reset villagers with max level).

3 new compasses that let you found the ancient city, woodland mansion and trial chamber respectly

Now Elytras use more durability when flying and MUCH more when using fireworks.

Mending needs more XP.
