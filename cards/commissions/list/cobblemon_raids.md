### Cobblemon Raids

![Cobblemon Raids](imgs/cobblemon_raids_img.png)

Invite and create a party of 3 players to start a raid!! Only 3 lives for team, each loss costs a life, and you need to fight through 3 Pokémon and then fight the boss (normally a legendary/special Pokémon) with special abilities. After defeating it, you have the chance to capture it. GUIs, config, and everything was used here!!.

You can define the catch rate after defeating the boss, setting the boss and normal pokemons to fight all with the config, setting a min. and max. battle, and much more.
